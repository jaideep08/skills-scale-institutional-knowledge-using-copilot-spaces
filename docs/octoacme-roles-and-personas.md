# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Professional

### Role Summary
QA and testing professionals validate that features meet acceptance criteria, identify defects early, and support high-quality releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans for unit, integration, and end-to-end coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria against product and engineering requirements
- Partner with developers to reproduce defects and confirm fixes
- Track quality risks, open issues, and release readiness
- Support regression planning and smoke tests before release

### Goals
- Catch defects before release and reduce production risk
- Improve confidence in delivery quality
- Align testing with business value and user impact

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA readiness reviews
- Defect triage and release sign-off discussions
- Cross-functional check-ins with PMs, developers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation and testability
- Provides evidence to Product Managers and Project Managers on release readiness and risk
- Helps Stakeholders understand quality status and any trade-offs in delivery

---

## Product Lead / Director

### Role Summary
Product Leads or Directors provide strategic product direction, align cross-functional teams, and sponsor the product vision across multiple initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and product priorities across programs
- Align the roadmap with business goals, customer needs, and resource constraints
- Review major trade-offs and help resolve cross-team conflicts
- Sponsor stakeholder alignment and support milestone decisions
- Set expectations for outcome measurement and business impact

### Goals
- Ensure strategic alignment across the portfolio
- Drive product value and measurable outcomes
- Support sustainable delivery by clarifying priorities and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and executive briefings
- Strategic roadmap and prioritization reviews
- Escalation discussions with PMs, Product Managers, and sponsors

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate strategy into backlog priorities
- Supports Project Managers in identifying scope, sequencing, and dependency trade-offs
- Advises Developers on product intent and expected customer outcomes

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and executive stakeholders authorize the initiative, provide business sponsorship, and ensure the project remains aligned to strategic goals and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and business case
- Allocate budget, staffing, and executive support
- Make final escalation decisions when project risk or priority is disputed
- Champion stakeholder alignment and cross-functional support
- Review major milestones and business outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with strategic priorities and ROI
- Support delivery by clearing barriers and funding needed resources
- Maintain executive visibility into key risks and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Project approval and milestone reviews
- Escalation updates during major risks or business-impacting issues
- Leadership briefings and investment decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides authority and strategic context to Project Managers and Product Leads
- Reviews progress and decisions with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Supports escalation paths when risk, timeline, or stakeholder alignment requires executive intervention

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and compliance officers protect the organization from technical and governance risk by validating that projects follow secure, compliant, and resilient practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements during planning and design
- Partner with engineering teams to assess vulnerabilities and mitigation steps
- Support secure development practices and compliance checks in CI/CD processes
- Manage incident response coordination for security events
- Ensure projects meet safety, compliance, and policy obligations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across delivery efforts
- Support a secure release process and strong operational resilience
- Build trust with customers, stakeholders, and leadership

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design checkpoints
- Incident response updates and compliance status reporting
- Collaboration with project leads on risk mitigation and release gating

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to identify and remediate security issues
- Provides risk insights to Project Managers and Sponsors during governance reviews
- Helps Product Managers assess business risk and release impact of security decisions

---

## Stakeholder / Cross-functional Partner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and cross-functional partners represent business units, dependent teams, or adjacent functions that contribute to project success and must remain aligned throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on requirements, constraints, and business priorities
- Communicate dependencies, decisions, and handoffs across teams
- Validate deliverables against user needs and operational realities
- Escalate risks, delays, or conflicts that affect delivery outcomes
- Support adoption and rollout decisions when relevant to their domain

### Goals
- Keep delivery connected to broader organizational priorities
- Reduce surprises and rework across dependent teams
- Improve alignment and customer readiness at launch

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff meetings and requirement reviews
- Dependency tracking and milestone check-ins
- Release readiness and adoption feedback loops

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides context to Product Managers and Project Managers for prioritization and planning
- Partners with Developers and QA on feature readiness and validation scenarios
- Supports Sponsors and Product Leads by highlighting operational or business impact

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify ownership, communication paths, and decision-making across product, delivery, and governance activities.
